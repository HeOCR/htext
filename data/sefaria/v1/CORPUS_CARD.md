# Sefaria historical texts v1

6,340 unpointed lines and one pointed line from 47 chapters of two exact historical editions. The Vienna 1887 Schechter Recension A of *Avot DeRabbi Natan* and the Warsaw 1884 edition of *Sefer Yetzirah* carry the Public Domain label in Sefaria's exact-version metadata. The line indexes bind each output line to its chapter and source segment.
